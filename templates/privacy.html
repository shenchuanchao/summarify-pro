Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 16, 2026

Thank you for choosing Summarify Pro ("we", "us", "our"), an AI document summarization tool designed to help you efficiently process and summarize documents. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use our website and services (the "Service"). By using Summarify Pro, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide and improve our Service. The information we collect falls into two categories:

1.1 Personal Information

  • Account Information: When you register an account, we collect your email address and password (encrypted). We do not collect your real name, phone number, or other sensitive personal information unless you voluntarily provide it.
  • Payment Information: If you subscribe to our Premium Plan, we use Stripe (a third-party payment processor) to handle your payment. We do not store your credit card number, CVV, or other payment details; all payment information is managed by Stripe.

1.2 Non-Personal Information

  • Document Data: When you upload documents (PDF, Word, etc.) or enter URLs to use our summarization service, we temporarily process the content of these documents to generate AI summaries. We DO NOT store your original documents or their content after the summarization process is completed.
  • Usage Data: We collect non-identifiable data about how you use our Service, including the number of documents processed, the type of functions used (summary, key points, translation), and your device/browser information (e.g., browser type, operating system). This data is used to improve our Service and analyze user behavior.
  • Cookies: We use cookies to remember your login status and preferences. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but this may affect the functionality of the Service.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain our Service (e.g., process document summarization, manage your account, and process payments).
  • To improve our Service (e.g., analyze usage data to optimize performance, fix bugs, and add new features).
  • To communicate with you (e.g., send account-related notifications, service updates, or promotional offers, if you opt-in).
  • To ensure the security of our Service (e.g., detect and prevent fraud, unauthorized access, or abuse).

3. Data Storage and Security

  • Data Storage: We store your personal information (email, encrypted password) on secure servers provided by Supabase, which uses industry-standard encryption (SSL/TLS) to protect data in transit and at rest.
  • Document Handling: As mentioned, we do not store your original documents or their content. All document data is temporarily processed and deleted immediately after the AI summarization is completed.
  • Security Measures: We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services to help us provide and improve our Service. These third parties have access to your information only to perform specific tasks on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose.

  • Stripe: For payment processing. Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Supabase: For data storage and user management. Supabase's privacy policy.
  • Zhipu AI / DeepSeek: For AI summarization services. Their privacy policies: Zhipu AI and DeepSeek.

5. Your Rights

Under applicable privacy laws (e.g., GDPR, CCPA), you have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access: You can request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to Correction: You can request to correct any inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
  • Right to Deletion: You can request to delete your personal information and account at any time.
  • Right to Opt-Out: You can opt out of receiving promotional emails by clicking the "unsubscribe" link in the email.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at veichao2022@outlook.com. We will respond to your request within a reasonable timeframe.

6. Data Retention

  • We retain your account information (email, encrypted password) for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal information within 30 days.
  • We retain non-personal usage data for 12 months to improve our Service, after which it is anonymized and used for statistical purposes.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on our website. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at:

  • Website: https://summarifypro.com
  • Email: veichao2022@outlook.com

This Privacy Policy applies only to the Service provided by Summarify Pro. It does not apply to third-party websites or services that you may access through our Service.
