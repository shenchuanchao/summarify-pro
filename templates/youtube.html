Frequently Asked Questions

Which YouTube videos can I summarize?

Any YouTube video that has captions (manual or auto-generated). Most videos on YouTube have auto-generated captions, so the vast majority will work.

Is the YouTube summarizer free?

Free users get 10 AI actions per day. Upgrade to Premium for unlimited access — no daily limits on any feature.

Can I translate YouTube summaries to other languages?

Yes! After fetching the transcript, click "Translate to" and choose from Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, French, German, Arabic, and more.

What if a video has no transcript?

We'll show a clear error message. You can try another video — most popular videos have transcripts available.
