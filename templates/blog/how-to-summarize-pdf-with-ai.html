What Makes a Good AI Summary?

Not all AI summarizers are created equal. Here is what separates the good ones from the rest:

Accuracy Over Compression

A good summary preserves meaning, not just word count. The best tools use large language models (like GLM-4 and DeepSeek) that understand context and nuance — they don't just pick random sentences.

Structured Output

Look for tools that organize information logically: introduction, main findings, supporting evidence, and conclusions. Summarify Pro structures its summaries this way so they read like a human wrote them.

Multi-Format Support

Your documents come in different formats. A good AI tool should handle PDFs, Word documents, URLs, and even YouTube videos — all in one place.
