5 Best Free YouTube Video Summarizers in 2026 (Tested & Ranked)

A 1-hour YouTube lecture, summarized in 30 seconds. We put the top free tools to the test on long-form videos across multiple genres.

Why Use a YouTube Video Summarizer?

YouTube is the world's second-largest search engine, but video is inherently slow to consume. A 45-minute lecture takes 45 minutes to watch. An hour-long podcast is a full commute. When you need the information but not the runtime, a YouTube summarizer extracts the key points in seconds.

Ideal Use Cases

  • Students — Summarize recorded lectures and educational content before exams
  • Lifelong learners — Get the gist of long-form documentaries and talks
  • Content creators — Research competitors' videos quickly
  • Professionals — Review conference talks and training videos efficiently

How We Tested

We tested each tool on the same three videos: a 20-minute educational lecture, a 45-minute tech podcast, and a 5-minute news clip. Criteria: accuracy, speed, ease of use, language support, and whether a free tier actually delivers value.

The Rankings

#1: Summarify Pro

Our own YouTube Summarizer took the top spot for one reason: it actually understands the video content, not just the transcript. Powered by GLM-4 and DeepSeek, it produces summaries that are structured, accurate, and readable.

  • Best for: All-around YouTube summarization across any video type
  • Free tier: 10 summaries/day with account, 3 without
  • Languages: 5 (EN, ZH, ES, DE, JA)
  • Standout feature: Offers AI Summary, Key Points bullet extraction, and full-text translation
  • Rating: ★★★★★ (4.8/5)

#2: NoteGPT

NoteGPT is a solid Chrome extension that generates summaries and mind maps from YouTube videos. It captures timestamps and key moments well, though the free tier is limited to 5 summaries per month.

  • Best for: Visual learners who want mind maps alongside text
  • Free tier: 5 summaries/month
  • Standout feature: AI-generated mind maps and timestamped highlights
  • Rating: ★★★★☆ (4.2/5)

#3: YouTube Summarizer by Eightify

Eightify is a popular Chrome extension with support for 40+ languages. It produces reasonably accurate summaries but has become increasingly aggressive with its paywall — most features now require a subscription.

  • Best for: Quick, short summaries in many languages
  • Free tier: Very limited (mostly paywalled)
  • Standout feature: 40+ language support
  • Rating: ★★★☆☆ (3.5/5)

#4: YouTube Digest (ChatGPT Plugin)

If you already have ChatGPT Plus, you can use it to summarize YouTube transcripts. Quality is inconsistent — it works great on some videos and misses the point entirely on others. The lack of a dedicated UI means copy-pasting URLs manually.

  • Best for: Existing ChatGPT users who want an all-in-one workflow
  • Free tier: None (requires ChatGPT Plus)
  • Standout feature: Conversation-style follow-up questions
  • Rating: ★★★☆☆ (3.3/5)

#5: Glarity

Glarity is a free open-source extension that summarizes YouTube videos alongside Google search results. It covers the basics but struggles with longer videos (30+ minutes) where summaries become vague and miss key details.

  • Best for: Casual users who want summaries alongside search results
  • Free tier: Fully free
  • Standout feature: Google search integration + YouTube sidebar
  • Rating: ★★★☆☆ (3.0/5)
